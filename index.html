Tournament Format

How the CSA Club Team Championships work

9
Players per team

Each team fields 9 players in ladder order. A minimum of 7 is required.

5
Wins needed for team victory

Best of 9 individual matches. First to 5 individual wins takes the team match.

3
Games to win an individual match

Best of 5 games, each played to 11 points (point-a-rally scoring).

3
Days of competition

Friday through Sunday, February 13–15 at the Arlen Specter Center.
